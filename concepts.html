Spotlight — six directions

Six different treatments for the Brief's spotlight, all running live with the real Oct 26 data. No progress bars, no dot-orb. Nothing reacts to the mouse in any of them.

1Cinema Full-bleed imagery with a slow Ken Burns drift, film credits text. The photography business shown photographically.
2Map Flyover A real St. Lawrence County map. The camera flies town to town; each stop is a spotlighted business where it actually is.
3Living Chart The metric itself is the visual: the follower line draws itself across the whole panel, a light rides it to today.

4Bar Race The week's movers as a race: bars grow and overtake each other, then settle into the final ranking. Pure data, always moving.

5Card Deck The six spotlights as physical cards on a rotating carousel. Tactile, deep, nothing abstract — each card is the business.
6The Market, Named The whole market as a labeled network — every node carries its business's name. The camera glides to each spotlighted name.
