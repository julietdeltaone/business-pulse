The orb — six versions

Same spotlight data, six different orbs. Each one is built from something real — a globe, a lens, terrain, orbits — and each reacts when the featured business changes (~4.5s). None of them react to the mouse.

1The Globe A true wireframe globe — meridians, parallels, a lit point for the featured business, and a great-circle arc drawn from the last stop to the next.
2Sonar A living core that broadcasts rings. Ring speed and reach follow the business's momentum — a +35 week literally pulses harder than a flat one.
3The Lens A camera aperture — right for a photography market. Blades rotate and the iris opens on each new business, revealing it inside the lens.
4Terrain North Country topography: layered contour rings turning slowly inside the orb, the featured business planted as a summit marker.
5The Current Hundreds of particles stream around the core like wind over the county. The stream bends and gathers into a bright cluster on the featured business.
6Orbit A glass sphere with businesses in orbit on tilted rings — rings are follower tiers. The featured business swings around to the front and swells.
